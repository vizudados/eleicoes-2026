FORMAT: landscape orientation, 3:2.
SUBJECT: One continuous Brazilian horizon that travels from Rio de Janeiro to Sao Paulo, read from left to right. LEFT HALF: Guanabara Bay in Rio de Janeiro: the Sugarloaf Mountain (Pao de Acucar) with its cable car line and a small cable car, smaller rounded granite hills beside it, a short curved beach with a few palm trees and low buildings, a couple of small sailboats on calm water lines; far behind, small on a peak, the Corcovado with the tiny Christ the Redeemer statue. The coast gradually becomes a city toward the middle. RIGHT HALF: the skyline of Sao Paulo: a dense cluster of modernist and contemporary towers, including one long building with a wavy S-shaped facade (Copan) and one tall slim tower (Edificio Italia), many apartment blocks with small windows and water tanks, and in front, over a calm river, a cable-stayed bridge with a tall X-shaped mast and fans of cables (Ponte Estaiada). Keep the skyline compact and not taller than the Sugarloaf.
COMPOSITION: A wide panoramic horizon strip. Everything stands on one perfectly straight horizontal ground line at about 85% of the image height that runs across the FULL width of the image, from the very left edge to the very right edge. The drawing is a low horizontal band in the lower half of the image; the tallest element reaches no higher than about 45% of the image height; empty white sky above. Toward the far left and far right edges the scene thins out to only low grass, stones and the ground line, so this panel can be joined side by side with other panels into one continuous horizon.
REFERENCES: Image 1 is the line-art style reference: use it ONLY for the pen stroke (line weight, sketchy quality, level of detail, how plants and buildings are drawn); do not copy its content.
CONSTRAINTS: Black ink lines on pure white only. No text, no letters, no numbers, no logos, no flags, no party symbols, no signage, no watermark, no signature, no border or frame. No people.
