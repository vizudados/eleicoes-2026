FORMAT: landscape orientation, 3:2.
SUBJECT: One continuous Brazilian horizon that travels from the Amazon to the semi-arid sertao, read from left to right. LEFT HALF: the edge of a wide Amazon river: a few wooden stilt houses (palafitas) with zinc roofs standing over the water, linked by a wooden walkway, a small dugout canoe, tall slender acai palms and a dense rainforest canopy with one giant kapok tree (sumauma) behind; calm horizontal lines for the river and its reflections. Everything stands on one perfectly straight horizontal ground line at about 85% of the image height that runs across the FULL width of the image, from the very left edge to the very right edge. Toward the far left and far right edges the scene thins out to only low grass, stones and the ground line, so this panel can be joined side by side with other panels into one continuous horizon.
